Where the data is and who processes it

The data runs on the infrastructure of the processors below; the primary database instance is located in the European Union. We do not sell data or pass it on for advertising. Further recipients are to the extent necessary, public authorities where the law requires it.

  • Cloudflare, Inc. — hosting of the app and the database (primary instance in the EU) (primary database instance in the EU) (data processing agreement)
  • Resend (Plus Five Five, Inc.) — sending account e-mails: the sign-in link, order confirmations, notices about the end of the trial and of prepaid periods, and answers to reports (USA / EU) (data processing agreement)
  • GitHub, Inc. (Microsoft) — keeping customers' reports for support (report text, technical details of the page and an anonymous account fingerprint with whether the subscription is paid and the account's age — encrypted, GitHub sees only an unreadable block; no e-mail, no name) (USA / EU) (data processing agreement)
  • Google Ireland Limited / Google Commerce Limited — selling the subscription in the Google Play app: Google takes and processes the payment; we learn only that a purchase exists and until when it is paid (EU)
  • Anthropic, PBC — automatically sorting reports and questions sent to support and answering ordinary questions with artificial intelligence (the report text and technical details of the page, no e-mail or name unless you write them into the text yourself); the data is not used to train models; more complex and legal matters are handled by a person (USA) (data processing agreement)

Support and artificial intelligence

Reports and questions you send us from the app are first read by our artificial intelligence assistant (a model by Anthropic). It sorts the reports and answers ordinary questions by itself from the description of the app; each of its answers is marked as such. Bugs, wishes, more complex and legal matters (withdrawal, refunds, data requests) are handled by a person. The assistant does not decide about your rights or your account. It sees the text of the report and technical details of the page, not your e-mail or name — unless you write them into the text yourself; so please do not put your own customers' personal data into a report.

Transfers outside the European Union

The processors listed are companies based in the United States. Although the primary database instance is in the EU, the data may be accessed from the United States during operation (in particular for support, protecting the service and delivering e-mail). Such a transfer is covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses under Article 46(2)(c) GDPR, which form part of the processing agreements linked above, or by the Commission's adequacy decision (EU–US Data Privacy Framework) where the processor is certified under it. We will provide a copy of the safeguards on request at the contact e-mail.

Cookies

We use only technical cookies necessary for the service — no tracking or advertising cookies. The law does not require consent for such cookies (Section 89(3) of Czech Act No. 127/2005 Coll., on Electronic Communications):

  • __Host-relace — keeping you signed in (technical, HttpOnly); valid for 30 days from the last use, at most 90 days.
  • aplikace — remembering that the page runs inside the Google Play app, so that it never offers another way of paying there (technical); valid for 1 year.

When you switch the language or the currency in the app yourself, your browser remembers that choice (browser storage, item sg-volba) so you need not repeat it. Nothing else is in it and it is never sent anywhere; you remove it by clearing the site's data in your browser. The default currency follows the country you connect from — our hosting provider knows it from the connection and we do not store it anywhere.

Your rights

  • Access to your data and a copy of it: download it in the app at any time (Account → Download my data).
  • Deleting the account and all data: in the app (Account → Delete account); the steps, also without the app, are on the page Deleting your account. Data is deleted from the live database immediately and from secured backups within 30 days, when the backups are overwritten. The copies of your reports at our provider GitHub are encrypted — only we can read them. When the account is deleted we replace their text with a deletion notice and delete our decrypted copy. The encrypted form of a report may, however, remain in the edit history GitHub keeps; it cannot be read without our key. We keep longer only accounting and tax documents where the law requires it.
  • You also have the right to rectification, restriction of processing and data portability — write to the contact e-mail above.
  • Right to object (Article 21 GDPR): you may object at any time, at the contact e-mail, to processing based on our legitimate interest — that is, to the operational and security records. We then stop unless we demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds to continue.
  • You may lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů), Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Prague 7 (uoou.gov.cz), or with the supervisory authority of your country.

We answer a request within one month at the latest; in complex cases this may be extended by two further months, and we will tell you so.

Security incidents

If a personal data breach occurred, we would notify the Office for Personal Data Protection within 72 hours of finding out, where it is likely to result in a risk to you. Where it is likely to result in a high risk to you, we would also inform you without undue delay. We have a written procedure for this.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; we announce a substantial change in the app or by e-mail at least 30 days in advance. Earlier versions are available on request.
