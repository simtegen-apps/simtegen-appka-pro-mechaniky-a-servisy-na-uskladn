Data processing agreement

Version 2.1 · effective from 24 November 2026 · earlier versions on request. This is a translation of the Czech version, which prevails in case of doubt.

Concluded under Article 28 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) between the customer of the service Pneusklad — evidence uskladněných pneu as the controller and SimteGen, s.r.o., company ID (IČO) 13980351, registered office Korunní 2569/108, Vinohrady, 101 00 Praha 10, Czech Republic, e-mail support@simtegen.com as the processor. It forms part of the terms of service.

1. Subject matter, nature, purpose and duration of the processing

Subject matter and nature: the processor stores and makes available for the controller the personal data the controller puts into the service. The processing is automated and includes storage in a database, display in the app, backups, export and erasure.

Purpose: solely running the service for the controller. The processor does not use the data for its own purposes, does not sell it and does not pass it on for advertising.

Duration: the processing lasts as long as the contract for the service and ends with erasure or return of the data under article 10.

This agreement covers the content the controller puts into the service. It does not cover data about the controller's account (e-mail address, sign-in tokens, sessions, subscription records, operational logs) — for those the processor is an independent controller and their processing is described in the privacy policy.

2. Categories of data subjects and of personal data

Data subjects: motorists whose tyres the shop stores or changes, and the shop's staff the administrator invites into the app.

Categories of personal data: name, phone and possibly e-mail of the motorist whose tyres the shop stores, the vehicle's registration plate and description, details of the stored sets (size, tread, tread depths, position in the store, price and whether storage is paid), workshop appointments and the content of notes the shop's staff fill in.

The exact scope is determined by the controller through the data it puts into the service. The controller is responsible for having a legal basis for processing it and for having properly informed the data subjects.

The service is not intended for special categories of data under Article 9 GDPR (data concerning health, biometric data and the like) or for data relating to criminal convictions under Article 10 GDPR. If the controller puts them in nevertheless, it does so at its own responsibility and must tell the processor in advance so that the adequacy of security can be assessed.

3. The controller's instructions

The processor processes the data only on documented instructions from the controller; using the service under the terms of service and this agreement is such an instruction. The controller sends further instructions to the contact e-mail; for an instruction beyond the ordinary running of the service, the parties may agree on covering the necessary costs.

If the processor considers an instruction to infringe the GDPR or other data protection law, it tells the controller without delay and need not carry it out until it is clarified.

The data is processed in the European Union. The processor transfers it outside the EU only on the controller's instruction or on the basis of EU or Member State law; in that case it informs the controller in advance unless that law prohibits it on important grounds of public interest. Transfers related to engaging further processors are governed by article 6.

4. Confidentiality

Only persons who have committed themselves to confidentiality by contract or are under a statutory obligation of confidentiality have access to the data. The obligation continues after their involvement in the processing and after this agreement ends.

5. Security

Taking into account the state of the art, the nature of the processing and the risks, the processor applies in particular these technical and organisational measures (Article 32 GDPR):

  • encrypted transfer (HTTPS) and encryption of data at rest by the infrastructure provider;
  • sign-in without passwords by one-time links — only digests of the tokens are stored in the database;
  • a separate database for each product with the primary instance in the EU and a separate test database (no production data is copied into the test database);
  • restricted and recorded access to the production environment, only for the persons who need it;
  • regular backups, overwritten within 30 days at the latest;
  • account deletion by the controller directly in the app, which deletes all its data;
  • a written procedure for security incidents and regular checks that the measures work.

The processor may change the measures but must not lower the level of security achieved.

6. Further processors

The controller gives general authorisation to engage further processors. Currently engaged are:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. — hosting of the app and the database (primary instance in the EU) (primary database instance in the EU) (data processing agreement)
  • Resend (Plus Five Five, Inc.) — sending account e-mails: the sign-in link, order confirmations, notices about the end of the trial and of prepaid periods, and answers to reports (USA / EU) (data processing agreement)
  • GitHub, Inc. (Microsoft) — keeping customers' reports for support (report text, technical details of the page and an anonymous account fingerprint with whether the subscription is paid and the account's age — encrypted, GitHub sees only an unreadable block; no e-mail, no name) (USA / EU) (data processing agreement)
  • Google Ireland Limited / Google Commerce Limited — selling the subscription in the Google Play app: Google takes and processes the payment; we learn only that a purchase exists and until when it is paid (EU)
  • Anthropic, PBC — automatically sorting reports and questions sent to support and answering ordinary questions with artificial intelligence (the report text and technical details of the page, no e-mail or name unless you write them into the text yourself); the data is not used to train models; more complex and legal matters are handled by a person (USA) (data processing agreement)

The processor imposes on each further processor by contract the same data protection obligations as it has under this agreement, in particular sufficient technical and organisational security. If a further processor fails to fulfil its obligations, the processor remains fully liable to the controller for its performance (Article 28(4) GDPR).

The processor announces an intended change or addition of further processors in the app or by e-mail at least 30 days in advance. The controller may object within that period; if no agreement is reached, it may end the contract for the service as of the day the change takes effect, without penalty and with a pro-rata refund of the subscription.

Transfers outside the EU within the further processors listed are covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses under Article 46(2)(c) GDPR, which form part of the linked processing agreements, or by an adequacy decision. The processor provides a copy of the safeguards on request.

7. Assistance

The processor assists the controller:

  • with data subjects' requests (Article 28(3)(e) GDPR) — a copy, rectification and erasure of data are available to the controller directly in the app; for other requests the processor assists by e-mail without undue delay, within 5 working days at the latest. If a data subject turns to the processor directly, the processor does not handle the request and forwards it to the controller without undue delay;
  • with the obligations under Articles 32 to 36 GDPR — security, breach notification, impact assessment and prior consultation, taking into account the nature of the processing and the information available to it.

8. Demonstrating compliance and audits

The processor makes available to the controller all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with Article 28 GDPR and allows for audits, including inspections, by the controller or an auditor mandated by it, and contributes to them (Article 28(3)(h) GDPR).

Practical conditions: an audit is announced at least 14 days in advance, takes place in working hours, must not disrupt the operation unreasonably and the auditor is bound by confidentiality. At most one audit a year is free of charge; this does not apply to an audit after a security incident or on the instruction of a supervisory authority. These conditions do not limit the controller's right to an audit, they only govern how it is carried out.

9. Personal data breach

If the processor becomes aware of a personal data breach concerning the controller's data, it notifies the controller without undue delay, within 24 hours of becoming aware of it at the latest, so that the controller can meet its 72-hour deadline under Article 33 GDPR. The notification describes the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of data subjects and records concerned, the likely consequences and the measures taken or proposed. Where not all information is available at once, the processor provides it in phases without further undue delay.

The processor does not notify breaches concerning the controller's data to the supervisory authority or to data subjects — that is the controller's obligation, unless the controller expressly asks the processor to do so.

10. Termination, erasure and return of data

When the provision of the service ends, the processor, at the controller's choice, either deletes the personal data or returns it, and deletes existing copies (Article 28(3)(g) GDPR). The controller states its choice by the end of the transition period under article 6 of the terms of service at the latest; if it does not, the processor deletes the data after that period.

Return means an export in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format, available to the controller directly in the app. By deleting the account in the app the controller chooses erasure.

Data is deleted from the live database immediately and from backups within 30 days, when they are overwritten. Data is kept longer only where EU or Member State law requires it; the processor informs the controller of such a case.

11. Liability and final provisions

The parties' liability is governed by Article 82 GDPR and the terms of service; a limitation of compensation agreed in the terms of service does not apply to obligations under this agreement breached intentionally or by gross negligence.

This agreement is governed by Czech law. If any of its provisions conflicts with the GDPR, the GDPR applies and the other provisions remain in force.
